Cookies Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Cookies are small text files saved by websites in your browser. Tokiidea does not currently set first-party cookies and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The shop does use essential browser storage, such as local storage and session storage, for the functions below.

Storage used by this website

  • Shopping cart (local storage): Keeps the items and quantities in your cart in this browser between visits. It remains until you clear it or remove the items.
  • Product image choice (local storage): Carries the selected Kori image from the collection to the product page. It remains until replaced or cleared.
  • Page transition and notice display (session storage): Supports the product-page transition and remembers that you dismissed the storage notice for this browser tab. It is cleared when the tab's browser session ends.

Payment and external services

If you proceed to checkout, you are redirected to Stripe's hosted checkout. Stripe may use cookies or similar technologies needed to provide and secure its payment service. Those technologies are operated by Stripe and are subject to Stripe's cookie information.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. This makes a request to Google's servers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address. Tokiidea does not use Google Fonts for analytics or advertising.

Your choices

Because this website currently uses only storage needed for its core functions, the notice is informational and does not ask you to consent to analytics or advertising cookies. You can dismiss it with “Got it” and reopen it here. You can also clear or block browser storage in your browser settings; doing so may remove your cart or affect parts of the site.
