Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my plushie?

Each plushie is made to order, so please allow 3-5 business days for creation before shipping. Shipping times vary by location (see our Shipping Policy for details).

Can I wash my plushie?

Our plushies are surface washable. We recommend spot cleaning with a mild detergent and air drying. Avoid submerging in water or machine washing to preserve the quality.

Do you accept custom orders?

Yes! We love creating custom plushies. Please contact us via the Contact page with your ideas, and we'll discuss feasibility, pricing, and timeline.

What materials do you use?

We use hypoallergenic plush exterior fabrics and soft polyester filling. All materials are chosen for softness, durability, and safety.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide! International shipping times and costs vary. Please note that customers are responsible for any customs/duties fees imposed by their country.

What is your return policy?

As made-to-order items, we cannot accept returns for change of mind. However, if your plushie arrives damaged, please contact us within 3 days with photos for a replacement or refund. See our Returns & Refunds Policy for full details.
