Shipping Policy

Making Time: Each plushie is made to order. Please allow around 5 business days for us to make it before dispatch.

Shipping Method: Orders within Slovenia are sent by Pošta Slovenije using standard shipping. Tracking availability is not currently confirmed.

Estimated Delivery: Please allow approximately 3-5 business days for shipping after dispatch. Delivery times are estimates.

Shipping Destinations: Online checkout is currently available for delivery addresses in Slovenia only. If you are outside Slovenia, please contact us directly before ordering to ask whether shipping to your location can be arranged.

Shipping Cost: Standard shipping within Slovenia is €2.50 per order.

Other Notes:

  • Please double-check that your delivery address is correct.
  • Allow extra time during holidays or other busy periods.

Questions? Send us a carrier pigeon or just email us - we're here to help!
