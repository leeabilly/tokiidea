# Policies Guidance for Tokiidea

Since you're just getting started with your handmade plushie business, here are simple, clear policy templates you can customize. These cover the essentials while being friendly and approachable - matching your brand's whimsical yet professional vibe.

## 1. Shipping Policy
**Processing Time**: All plushies are made to order with love. Please allow 3-5 business days for creation before shipping.  
**Shipping Methods**: We use [your preferred carrier, e.g., USPS Priority Mail] with tracking provided.  
**Delivery Estimates**:  
- Domestic: 3-7 business days after shipment  
- International: 7-21 business days after shipment (varies by country)  
**Shipping Costs**: Calculated at checkout based on location and package weight.  
**Important Notes**:  
- We're not responsible for customs/duties on international orders  
- Address accuracy is crucial - please double-check before submitting  
- During holiday seasons, allow extra time for processing and shipping  

## 2. Returns & Refunds Policy
**Handmade Nature**: Each plushie is unique and made just for you.  
**Damaged Items**: If your plushie arrives damaged, please contact us within 3 days with photos - we'll gladly replace it or issue a refund.  
**Changes of Mind**: As made-to-order items, we cannot accept returns for change of mind. However, please contact us if you have any concerns - we want you to love your Tokiidea!  
**Exceptions**: We'll work with you on a case-by-case basis for genuine issues.  
**Refund Process**: Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

## 3. Privacy Policy
**What We Collect**:  
- Order information (name, address, email, phone) for fulfillment  
- Payment information (securely processed by Stripe - we don't store card details)  
- Email address if you subscribe to updates  
**How We Use It**:  
- To process and ship your order  
- To communicate about your order status  
- To send occasional updates about new plushies (only if you opt-in)  
**Sharing Information**:  
- We only share necessary info with shipping carriers to deliver your package  
- We never sell or rent your personal information  
**Your Rights**: You can request to see, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us.

## 4. Terms of Service
**By purchasing from Tokiidea, you agree to:**  
- Providing accurate shipping and contact information  
- Understanding that colors may vary slightly due to screen settings and handmade nature  
- Allowing 3-5 business days for order processing before shipment  
- Communicating respectfully with our small business  
**Intellectual Property**: All designs, photos, and content are original creations of Tokiidea and protected by copyright.  
**Governing Law**: These terms are governed by the laws of [your state/country].

## 5. Cookies Policy
**What are cookies?** Small text files stored on your browser to improve website experience.  
**What we use**:  
- Essential cookies: For site functionality (shopping cart, etc.)  
- Analytics: To understand how visitors use our site (via [Google Analytics/plausible/etc if used])  
- Preferences: To remember your settings  
**Your Control**: You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect site functionality.

## Customization Tips for Tokiidea:
1. **Voice**: Keep it warm, friendly, and slightly playful - like you're chatting with a friend who makes cute things
2. **Brand Touch**: Keep support copy warm, friendly, and in line with Tokiidea's voice.
3. **Specifics**: Replace bracketed info like [your preferred carrier] and [your state/country]
4. **Length**: These are intentionally concise for a small handmade business - you can expand as you grow
5. **Legal Note**: For maximum protection, consider consulting a small business lawyer as you scale, but these are great to start with!
